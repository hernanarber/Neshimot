Breath-hold check

The “control pause” — a simple gauge of breathing efficiency used in Buteyko-based training.

  1. Sit quietly and breathe normally through the nose for a minute.
  2. After a normal exhale, pinch your nose and press Start.
  3. Press Stop at the first clear urge to breathe — not your maximum. Your next breath should be calm.
