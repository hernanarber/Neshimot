Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Neshimot is a free breathing-practice website run by ArberTech. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your name and email, when you unlock the practices after your first session.
  • Whether you'd like occasional emails — only if you tick the box. It is unticked by default.
  • Which language you signed up in (English or Hebrew), so any email reaches you in that language.

Your practice history, breathing diary notes, streak, breath-hold results and settings are stored only in your own browser. They never reach us. You can download your diary at any time, and clear everything with “Clear my practice history”.

Why we collect it

  • To give you access to all practices (that's what your sign-up does).
  • If you opted in, to send occasional breathing tips and updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

We never sell or rent your details.

Who processes it

  • HubSpot stores your name, email and email preference in our contact list.
  • Vercel hosts the website and keeps standard server logs for security. Vercel Web Analytics counts page visits anonymously — without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Google Fonts serves the website's typefaces.

We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or — for email — until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent to emails at any time. Write to hernan@arbertech.com and we'll take care of it.
